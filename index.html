Manifesto · Chapter I

We work for clients who would rather make one thing that lasts than ten that don't.

What we do

Lumen is an eight-person studio working at the seam of brand systems, moving image and engineered web. We partner with founders, cultural institutions and small product teams who treat design as a long, patient practice.

Each engagement begins with weeks of listening — references, archives, conversations in person — and ends with a system you can grow into for years.

How we work

We take on six engagements a year. Small teams, long timelines, generous edit rounds. We share working files instead of polished decks, and we keep the prototype open the whole way through.

Marks, type, motion and code, finished with a quiet manual you can keep extending years after we're done.

Approach · Chapter IV

We work in four slow movements. Each is open to revision, and none of them happens in a hurry.

  1. Listen

    Weeks of conversation. We trade references, archives, sketches and the questions we haven't answered yet.

  2. Frame

    A one-page written direction — the spine that every visual and editorial decision will hang from for the rest of the project.

  3. Make

    Slow iterations in small batches. We share working files, not polished decks, and edit in public with you, weekly.

  4. Hand off

    A complete system — type, motion, code and a quiet manual you can keep extending years after we're gone.
